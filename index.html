Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer custom designs?

Yes, all fabrication plans are customized to client specifications, site load requirements, and architectural blueprints.

What is your typical project timeline?

Standard structures take 4-6 weeks from initial design and detailing approval to factory manufacture and final site erection.

What certifications do your steel and welds have?

We use certified IS 2062/ASTM A572 steel, and our welding team holds AWS D1.1 certifications, ensuring supreme structural strength.
