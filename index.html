Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer custom designs?

Yes, all fabrication plans are customized to client specifications, site load requirements, and architectural blueprints. We generate detailed CAD plans prior to manufacture.

What is the typical project timeline?

Standard structures (like PEB warehouses) take 4-6 weeks from initial design and detailing approval to factory manufacture at Hebbagodi and final site erection.

What certifications do your steel and welds have?

We use certified high-strength steel (IS 2062 or ASTM A572 equivalent). Our welders are AWS D1.1 certified, and we provide material test reports (MTRs) on request.

Do you undertake works outside of Bangalore?

Yes. While our primary fabrication facility is in Hebbagodi (Bangalore), we regularly execute structural steel fabrication and PEB erections across Karnataka.

Can you manufacture heavy motorized shutters for wide industrial bays?

Yes. We manufacture heavy-duty motorized rolling shutters up to 30 feet in width with high-torque motors, heavy guides, and wind-resistant galvanized slats.
