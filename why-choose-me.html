Why Choose Me

Decades of structural expertise, hands-on engineering guidance, and a relentless commitment to quality fabrication.

A C Manjunatha

Proprietor & Chief Engineer

"For over four decades, I have personally overseen the design, fabrication, and installation of premium steel structures across Karnataka. My philosophy is simple: engineering must prioritize safety, reliability, and precision."

Under my leadership, M.M. Engineering Works has grown from a local workshop into Bangalore's premier fabricator of Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEBs), motorized rolling shutters, heavy security gates, and custom stainless steel and aluminum fittings.

I collaborate directly with architects, site owners, and project leads to deliver customized fabrication blueprints, ensuring each installation satisfies structural load requirements and site-specific dimensions.

Core Commitments

Direct Hands-On Oversight

Every blueprint, calculation sheet, and fabrication stage is personally inspected and approved by me to avoid any structural errors.

Premium Certified Materials

We only fabricate using tested IS 2062 grade steel, premium coatings, and certified AWS D1.1 welders to ensure lifelong structural strength.

On-Time Project Delivery

We value your construction timeline. Our manufacturing workflow is structured to hit milestones and deliver frameworks to the site on schedule.
