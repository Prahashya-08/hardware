Structural Steel

Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB) & Industrial Sheds

M.M. Engineering Works specializes in the turnkey engineering, fabrication, and erection of high-durability Pre-Engineered Steel Buildings. From massive manufacturing sheds to clean commercial warehouses, our structural design process ensures efficient load distribution, safety compliance, and maximum usable clear span space.

Technical Specifications

Steel Grade: ASTM A572 / IS 2062 E250/E350
Clear Span: Up to 40 meters (Col-free)
Roofing Sheet: 0.47mm - 0.55mm Galvalume AZ150
Welding Standard: AWS D1.1 certified welders

Industrial Doors

Heavy-Duty Rolling Shutters & Industrial Doors

Our custom-engineered rolling shutters are built to withstand high cycles of operation and offer robust protection for factories, godowns, shops, and logistics hubs. We offer manual gear-assisted systems as well as high-torque automated motorized rolling shutters with remote control and manual overrides for emergency operations.

Technical Specifications

Lath Profile: 18 / 20 / 22 SWG Galvanized Steel
Operation: Motorized (0.5 HP to 2.0 HP) / Manual
Motor Brand: Premium heavy-load side-mounted motors
Locking: Interlocking endlocks, heavy bottom plate
