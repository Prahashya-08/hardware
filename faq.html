Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our manufacturing capabilities, structural standards, timelines, or delivery? Find your answers below.

Do you offer custom fabrication designs?

Yes, all our fabrication blueprints are fully customized. We study your site constraints, loading requirements, and architect's layout, then generate detailed CAD plans prior to commencing factory manufacture.

What is the typical project timeline from order to installation?

Standard structural projects (such as warehouses or PEB extensions) usually take 4 to 6 weeks. This includes detailed design approval, off-site steel fabrication in our Hebbagodi factory, and final erection at your location.

What material grades and certifications do you support?

We work exclusively with certified high-strength steel (typically IS 2062 or ASTM A572 equivalent). Our welders hold AWS D1.1 certifications, and we can provide physical test reports (MTRs) and weld quality checks on request.

Do you undertake works outside of Bangalore?

Yes, while our primary manufacturing facility is located in Hebbagodi (Bangalore), we regularly execute structural steel fabrication and PEB erections across Karnataka and neighboring districts.

Can you manufacture heavy motorized shutters for wide industrial bays?

Yes. We manufacture heavy-duty motorized rolling shutters up to 30 feet in width. These are fitted with high-torque side-mounted motors, heavy guide channels, and high-strength galvanized steel slats to ensure safety and wind resistance.
